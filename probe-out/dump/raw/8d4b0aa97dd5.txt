						<h2>Recall of a batch of Bialetti Perfetto Moka Caramello coffee due to the elevated level of Ochratoxin A</h2>

						<p class="date">Friday, 23 October 2026</p>

						<table>
							<tr>
								<th colspan="2">Alert Summary</th>
							</tr>
								<tr>
									<td><strong>Category 1:</strong></td>
									<td>For Action</td>
								</tr>
															<tr>
									<td><strong>Alert Notification:</strong></td>
									<td>2026.68</td>
								</tr>
																						<tr>
									<td><strong>Product Identification:</strong></td>
									<td>Bialetti Perfetto Moka Caffe Macinato Caramello; pack size: 250 g&nbsp;
</td>
								</tr>
															<tr>
									<td><strong>Batch Code</strong></td>
									<td>LC5 BD149T; best before date: 29/05/2027</td>
								</tr>
																						<tr>
									<td><strong>Country Of Origin:</strong></td>
									<td>Italy</td>
								</tr>
						</table>

													<p><strong>Message:</strong><br /> <p>The above batch of Bialetti Perfetto Moka Caramello ground coffee is being recalled due to the elevated level of Ochratoxin A. Recall notices will be displayed at point-of-sale.&nbsp;</p>
</p>

							<p><strong>Nature Of Danger:</strong><br /> <p>The implicated batch contains Ochratoxin A above the legal maximum level.&nbsp; Ochratoxin A is a naturally occurring toxin produced by certain moulds.&nbsp; Ochratoxin A mainly forms after harvest during the storage of crops and foodstuffs.&nbsp; Although the consumption of the contaminated product does not pose an immediate risk to health, continuous consumption over a long period of time may pose a potential risk to health.&nbsp; Therefore, exposure to Ochratoxin A needs to be minimised.&nbsp;</p>
</p>


							<p><strong>Action Required:</strong><br /></p>
																			<p><strong>Manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors, caterers & retailers:</strong><br /> <p>Retailers are requested to remove the implicated batch from sale and display recall notices at point-of-sale.&nbsp;</p>
</p>
													<p><strong>Consumers:</strong><br /> <p>Consumers are advised not to use the implicated batch.</p>
</p>
