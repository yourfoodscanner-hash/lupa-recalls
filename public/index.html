LUPA – recall notices

This address serves one file to the LUPA app: official product recall notices, collected once a day from public sources and checked before they are published.

The file: v1/recalls.json

Every notice names its source and links to the official notice, which is the text that counts. The list may be incomplete: a product that is not on it is not necessarily safe.

Sources read automatically: RappelConso (France; data of the DGCCRF from data.economie.gouv.fr, Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0), lebensmittelwarnung.de (Germany), AGES (Austria), GOV.SI – Nevarni in neskladni izdelki (Slovenia), HAPIH – Obavijesti za potrošače (Croatia; notices of the State Inspectorate) the Food Safety Agency of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Fødevarestyrelsen (Denmark), Potraviny na pranýři (Czechia; SZPI and SVS), EFET (Greece) and, for the other EU member states, the European Commission’s RASFF Consumers’ Portal. Notices from other countries are entered by hand from the authorities’ own publications.
